The Homestay

A home by the lake with character

Located at Veslaška promenada 4 in the heart of Bled, Homestay Vito by Lake is a charming, family-run property that feels like a home away from home. With direct beachfront access, a lush garden, and a spacious terrace, it's the perfect base for exploring Slovenia's most beautiful lake.

The property offers a variety of rooms to suit every traveler — from cozy double rooms for couples to a three-bedroom vacation home that sleeps up to 10 guests. Each room features a private bathroom, air-conditioning, soundproofing, and lovely garden or city views.

What truly sets Homestay Vito apart is the hospitality. Guests consistently rave about Vito himself — a warm, attentive host who goes above and beyond to make every stay special. Whether it's a cold Radler in the fridge after a long bike ride or flexible luggage storage on check-out day, the little touches matter here.

Common questions

FAQ

How far is it to Lake Bled?

The homestay is just 100 m from the lake — about a 2-minute walk. Grajska Beach is a 16-minute walk away, and Bled Castle is about 1.6 km (1 mi) away.

Is parking available?

Yes — free private parking is available on site at no extra charge. No need to worry about finding a spot in busy Bled.

Is there a kitchen?

Yes — a fully equipped kitchen is available for guests who want to cook their own meals, with stove, oven, refrigerator, and dining area.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 3:00 PM to 8:00 PM, check-out from 7:00 AM to 10:00 AM. Please let us know your expected arrival time in advance — Vito is flexible and happy to accommodate.

Are pets allowed?

Unfortunately, pets are not allowed at Homestay Vito by Lake.

Is there WiFi?

Yes — free WiFi is available throughout the property, rated 9.5 by guests for its speed and reliability. Suitable for video calls and remote work.

What types of accommodation are available?

We offer a Small Double Room (1 full bed), a Quadruple Room (2 full beds, sleeps 4), a Ground Floor Apartment (queen bed + sofa bed, sleeps 4), and a Three-Bedroom Vacation Home (sleeps up to 10).

Is the property beachfront?

Yes — Homestay Vito by Lake offers direct beachfront access with a lush garden and a spacious terrace where you can relax after a day of exploring.
